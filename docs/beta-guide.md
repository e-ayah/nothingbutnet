# Beta Guide

## 1. What NothingButNet does
Teaches beginners how to improve in playing basketball. The app gives feedback on player's freethrow form based on clip uploaded by user.

## 2. Open the app: [nothingbutnet-op.vercel.app](https://nothingbutnet-op.vercel.app) (any phone or laptop). Make an account with any email.

## 3. Film a free throw: the filming-tips graphic 

[Filming Tips Graphic] (https://imgur.com/a/mw5vGN4)
- Film side ways
- Whole body and ball must be in frame
- Film in good lighting
- Must have only 1 person in frame
- Clip should be 5-10 seconds

## 4. Try this: upload 2–3 shots, read your results, check History.

## 5. Things we know: the first load can take a minute; analysis can take a few minutes. Form scores are still experimental — we're using your clips to improve them.
