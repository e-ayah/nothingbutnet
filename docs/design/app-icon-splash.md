### Design choices
For the app logo, I chose a simplistic black silhouette of a basketball player dunking, because it establishes the idea that this is a basketball teaching program. The color choices are black, orange, and different shades of grey to white. This color palette has a more active feel to it that would incentivize players to use this app when playing in real time.
